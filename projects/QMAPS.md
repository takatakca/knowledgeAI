# QMAPS

## Role
QMAPS is a reusable TAKATAK ecosystem capability for location/map/QR-oriented workflows.

## Architectural relationship
QMAPS should remain an independently bounded service/product while consuming central TAKATAK identity, permissions or shared services through contracts when required.

Avoid direct sibling-app database coupling.

## Reusable areas
- map/location records
- QR rendering
- location-aware links/actions
- future advertising/location targeting support
