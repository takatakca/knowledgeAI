# ON2GO

## Product direction

ON2GO.ca evolved from a marketplace concept into a Montréal-oriented local discovery/directory experience emphasizing late-night food and verified destinations.

## Current directory model

Public experience:
- hero / discovery
- categories
- Open Late
- Featured
- Trending
- Offers
- Tonight on ON2GO
- business profiles
- location hubs
- For Businesses

Business cards can include:
- business name and logo
- Open Late status
- official website
- official social accounts
- phone
- structured hours
- description
- delivery/order destination links

## Architecture principles

- Do not create fake listings.
- Official business imagery should link to official destinations when intended.
- Multi-brand and shared-location modeling is required.
- French root plus English route with proper hreflang support.
- TAKATAK can later provide centralized authentication/identity.

## Known ecosystem businesses

Examples tracked across the ecosystem include PPP Pizzeria, Pizza Inntime, Po Poulet, Gateau Montréal, Bin Molle Bin Dure, Nutrition Shake/Nutri Shake, Pi Pita, Café Bolon, O’Oeufs Déjeuner, O’Crêpe, Taco Mexican and Pita Libanais.
