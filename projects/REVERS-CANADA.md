# Revers Canada

## Mission
Revers Canada is a charitable/nonprofit project focused on women and children facing homelessness, with shelter, food, accompaniment and return-to-employment support.

## Digital/product needs
Relevant TAKATAK capabilities may include:
- website and campaign content
- donations/material-donation workflows
- communications
- volunteer/community information
- impact reporting
- multilingual content
- social publishing

## Editorial direction
Communication emphasizes dignity, listening and action. Impact metrics and public claims should be sourced and reviewable before publication.
