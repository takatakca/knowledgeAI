# REVpère

## Mission
REVpère is a Montréal-focused organization concept centered on supporting fathers through digital inclusion, employability and practical community support.

## Main product/service direction
- CV and job-search support
- digital skills
- web/AI tool literacy
- remote-work and collaboration tools
- group sessions
- directory/referral partnerships
- father-child activities
- community support programs

## TAKATAK relevance
The project can reuse TAKATAK capabilities for identity, content, directory/referral workflows, forms, communications and website operations while keeping its own mission-specific experience and data.
