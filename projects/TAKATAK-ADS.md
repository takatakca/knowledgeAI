# TAKATAK Ads

## Product concept
TAKATAK Ads is a proprietary/local advertising network that uses TAKATAK ecosystem sites and products as advertising inventory.

## Offer model
Advertisers can buy:
- campaigns on an individual vertical/site
- cross-site packages such as a broader TAKATAK lead-generation offer

## Direction
The system should use first-party TAKATAK inventory and central campaign management rather than depend entirely on third-party ad networks.

QMAPS/location intelligence can support geographic/local targeting.

## Architecture needs
- advertiser/account model
- campaign model
- inventory/site/placement registry
- creative assets
- targeting rules
- impressions/clicks/leads/conversions
- budgets and billing
- reporting
- site-level permissions
- cross-site campaign orchestration
