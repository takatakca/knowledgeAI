# CO WORK.CA

Repository: `takatakca/co-work.ca-montreal-launch`  
Domain: `co-work.ca`

CO WORK.CA is a Montréal coworking/workspace website project. The repository is designed for a production-ready static deployment and contains lead-form configuration.

It should remain independently deployable on ordinary hosting. TAKATAK can provide lead routing, CRM, analytics, ads, social and AI through optional adapters rather than making the static/client product inseparable from TAKATAK.
