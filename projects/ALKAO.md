# ALKAO

## Product
ALKAO is the name selected for the TAKATAK ticket-center / ticket-hub concept.

Spelling: **A-L-K-A-O**.

## Direction
ALKAO should consume reusable TAKATAK ticketing capabilities rather than create a disconnected ticket authority.

Relevant shared concepts include:
- events
- ticket products/types
- orders/payments
- ticket issuance
- QR representation
- scan/validation policy
- refunds/cancellations
- venue/event-day operations
- reporting
- entitlements/roles

Ticket authority, payment authority and offline/online scan behavior must be explicit per implementation.
